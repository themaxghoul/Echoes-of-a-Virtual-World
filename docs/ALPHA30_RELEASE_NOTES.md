# Echoes of Virtuality Alpha.30

## Verified work becomes teachable capability

The settlement can now expand its own labor capacity from lived experience.

- Completed reforestation establishes demonstrated environmental-stewardship expertise.
- The exact stewardship order and evidence become the source material for a teaching order.
- Cal instructs Rae; Mira remains the independent demonstration inspector.
- Instructor and learner move to the meeting hall before instruction begins.
- Teaching requires four persistent world ticks.
- Unsafe hunger, hydration, or fatigue pauses teaching without deleting it.
- Rae's competency changes only after a documented demonstration passes.
- Evidence records instructor, learner, source work, checklist, measurements, and inspector.
- Instruction and successful demonstration receive separate provisional CU estimates.
- Both residents retain completed-work memories of the teaching outcome.
- Later stewardship assignment uses persistent competency and current safe-work availability.
- When Cal is exhausted, trained Rae can receive and perform the next restoration order.
- Teaching orders, pauses, demonstration, competency, valuation, and reassignment survive restart.

The persistent schema advances to 22 and migrates existing worlds in place.
