# Alpha.24 source milestone

Alpha.24 makes the first player-created machine a continuing participant in world causality instead of a permanent modifier.

## Field technology lifecycle

- Each installed lever-pump cycle persists wear, condition, measured yield, and field history.
- Declining condition reduces actual water output; critical wear stops operation.
- Inspection and use can open one durable maintenance order tied to the installed design and causal evidence.
- Players may supply a wrench, timber brace, and replacement container/seal to complete the work.
- If the order remains open, Dev assigns Ada; Ada consumes settlement stores and Mira verifies the recovered condition.
- Missing inputs become persistent blockers and procurement demand.
- Maintenance demand outranks routine restocking: Orin selects a finite regional source, records the expedition and remaining stock, Dev verifies delivery, and the order retains its supply history.
- If no source exists, regional depletion remains visible on the order. Later natural regeneration or another material source can satisfy the same unresolved demand and allow Ada to resume work after a restart.
- Repair records evidence, remembered work, causal links, and balanced provisional CU valuation.
- Pump condition, failure, maintenance orders, assignments, and recovery survive authority restarts.

## Isometric observability

The well now displays a physical pump indicator, using normal, maintenance-due, and failed colors. Its inspector shows condition, field cycles, status, open order, assignee, required inputs, and blockers. The interaction button chooses inspection or maintenance from the authoritative state and the authenticated player's actual inventory.

Existing Alpha.23 archives do not contain these changes. The matching Alpha.24 portable executable and persistent-server archive have now been packaged and smoke-tested from extraction.

## Verified water enters public life

- Raw well water remains in the collecting player's authoritative inventory until physically submitted beside the measurement lab.
- A submitted batch consumes that inventory and retains a single-use chain of custody to the latest authoritative well-draw record.
- Two ticks later Mira tests volume and safety with a persistent evidence record. Pump condition at collection affects the result, so severely worn equipment can produce a rejected batch.
- Accepted water enters communal tested-water stock and creates an evidence-linked, balanced provisional contribution claim; rejected water produces no claim or usable supply.
- Every twelve ticks, the most dehydrated residents consume available verified water. Their changed needs and the depleted communal stock become the next simulation state.
- Batch status, rejection, valuation, resident consumption, shortages, and recovery all survive authority restart.
- A hydration shortage now opens autonomous public work. Emil is assigned, walks tile by tile to the well, records a physical collection, carries it to the laboratory, and waits for Mira's test.
- Failed autonomous samples do not end the obligation. If worn equipment caused the rejection, maintenance is opened; after Ada restores the pump, Emil retries the same historical work order and can deliver a safer batch.

## Survival actions interrupt and resume work

- Hungry residents walk to the communal kitchen and consume finite inspected meals. Each serving changes nutrition, belonging, stored portions, evidence, and later work capacity.
- Meal depletion persists as an affected-resident shortage and becomes council food pressure rather than spawning supplies.
- Fatigued residents travel to their household or recovery space and recover across multiple ticks.
- A worker interrupted during collection or delivery retains that exact phase. Recovery resumes the obligation without duplicating materials or erasing the interruption.

## Hunger commissions real kitchen work

- An unresolved meal shortage creates an autonomous cooking order with finite raw food, tested water, fuel, portions, cook, and inspector.
- Missing ingredients stop the order until measured procurement or verified water delivery supplies them.
- Assigned kitchen water is reserved from ordinary consumption; accepted public work cannot lose its inputs to an unrelated tick.
- Lena travels to the kitchen, preparation consumes ingredients and time, and Imani travels to inspect the resulting batch.
- Only accepted portions enter communal supply, establish recipe history, and create balanced provisional claims. Hungry residents then consume them and close the originating shortage.

## Meal batches age into history

- Accepted meals retain batch identity, remaining portions, preservation method, storage-derived shelf life, and expiration tick.
- Residents consume the batch closest to expiration, and consumption evidence keeps its custody link.
- Expired portions leave safe inventory and become persistent waste with storage measurements and council knowledge.
- Spoilage can create hunger, and hunger can commission replacement cooking during the same world tick.

## The kitchen learns from its losses

- Accumulated spoilage proposes a drying trial linked to the batches that were lost.
- The trial consumes food, fuel, a container, time, Lena's work, and Imani's inspection; shortages can delay it until procurement succeeds.
- A validated practice becomes cultural memory and receives evidence-linked provisional valuation.
- Future preserved cooking orders require an extra container and fuel, label their output as dried, and produce 24 additional shelf-life ticks.
