# Echoes of Virtuality Alpha.29

## Extraction now generates environmental responsibility

Finite tree sites no longer rely solely on passive regeneration.

- Tree stock at or below 25% capacity creates a persistent reforestation order.
- Stewardship begins only after the Founding Table, when the settlement can assume public obligations.
- Replanting requires two seed units and two units of tested water from finite stores.
- Missing inputs create durable blockers and no ecological benefit.
- Cal performs the restoration and Mira independently inspects it.
- Both NPCs move tile by tile to the actual depleted site and record their current region.
- Planting records inputs, tools, baseline stock, actors, inspector, and causal source.
- Ecological recovery takes twelve world ticks.
- Inspection measures before-and-after stock and restores no more than four surviving units.
- Verified restoration updates local stock, regional timber stock, and provisional market value.
- Verified labor and inspection produce provisional CU claims, not spendable currency.
- The order completes only after Cal and Mira return to the meeting hall.
- Survival needs can pause unsafe work without deleting the obligation.
- Orders, blockers, travel, evidence, valuation, memories, and recovery survive restart.

The persistent schema advances to 21 and migrates existing worlds in place.
