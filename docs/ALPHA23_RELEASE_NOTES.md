# EoV Alpha.23 test milestone

Alpha.23 joins the secured `Luciferous` owner identity to persistent creator proposals, evidence-gated public work, and the first reproducible player-engineering loop.

## Test focus

- Register ordinary desktop accounts and confirm they cannot see or read owner Jarvis memory.
- Provision `Luciferous` privately with `EOV_OWNER_PASSWORD`; confirm the account retains owner access across restart and rename because authority follows its UUID.
- Connect both services with the same `EOV_SESSION_SECRET` and set the persistent server's `EOV_OWNER_USER_ID` to the server-side Luciferous UUID.
- Submit a water directive from Jarvis and observe NPC review, council commissioning, inspection evidence, physical water work, provisional claims, and the following council decision.
- At the measurement lab, submit a dimensioned lever pump. Observe peer review, prototype material use, flow testing, independent reproduction, and tool-catalog validation.
- Check out a shovel, bucket, and wrench; complete the well, install the reproduced pump, and verify later water actions cite the design and outperform the baseline.
- Restart the persistent server during any lifecycle and confirm the same revision resumes without duplicate actions, rewards, prototypes, or technology units.

## Safety boundary

CU remains non-spendable provisional accounting. Deposits, withdrawals, conversions, and real-value payment behavior remain disabled. Alpha.23 is for local or trusted pre-alpha testing, not public untrusted hosting.
