# Echoes of Virtuality Alpha.27

## Residents generate social history without a player

The persistent settlement no longer needs a connected player to begin every meaningful conversation.

- Autonomous NPC speech runs from the authoritative world clock.
- A resident chooses a concern from current measured settlement pressure.
- Only nearby NPCs and players can hear the statement.
- Nearby NPCs preserve private memories identifying the speaker, concern, message, and tick.
- AI-originated statements use the same distinct-tick and multi-witness safeguards as player discourse.
- Sora records qualifying accumulated discussion as council knowledge and public pressure.
- Residents with no urgent survival or assigned-work obligation can later choose to discuss the remembered concern.
- Public pressure waits for an available council review and never performs direct physical changes.
- Messages, memories, claims, escalations, intentions, and institutional consequences survive restart.

Autonomous civic discourse begins after the Founding Table and at least one completed civic initiative. That gives residents lived institutional experience before their conversations can affect public prioritization.

The persistent schema advances to 19 and migrates existing worlds in place.
