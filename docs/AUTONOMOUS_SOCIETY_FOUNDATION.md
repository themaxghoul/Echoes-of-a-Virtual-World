## Perspective-dependent discovery

- **Story:** testimony, documents, language, rumor, and narrated records. It can reveal claims but not silently inspect physical truth.
- **Isometric:** spatial layout, construction progress, traffic, land use, and resource flows within the visible overview.
- **First person:** line of sight, sound, surface detail, nearby tool use, and occlusion. Close inspection reveals evidence unavailable from above.
- **VR backlog:** stereo depth, gesture, dexterity, body position, and embodied tool handling. VR is an input/perception adapter, not a separate world simulation.

All perspectives produce observations with provenance. Independent reproduction, instrumentation, or corroboration raises confidence. Merely changing cameras does not grant knowledge the entity did not perceive.

## Survival and thriving

Agents track nutrition, rest, shelter, safety, belonging, and purpose. Shortfalls affect priorities and decision quality. Life events apply pressure rather than scripted personality replacement. Thriving means maintaining needs while developing relationships, useful competence, agency, and long-term purpose.

Death ends new first-person testimony and action. Existing records, possessions, consequences, and other witnesses remain.

## Learning and specialization

Knowledge records competence, confidence, provenance, and reproducibility. Agents learn through observation, teaching, practice, experiments, and consequences. Teaching cannot transfer competence the teacher does not possess, and confident false knowledge remains possible until tested.

Specialization uses a nonlinear cognitive budget. An agent can broaden slowly, but deep mastery in many unrelated fields becomes expensive. This produces differentiated people: growers, builders, metallurgists, researchers, carers, logisticians, negotiators, and other roles emerge from needs and experience rather than fixed castes.

An eventual general intelligence is a developmental possibility, not a spawn flag. It would have to demonstrate transfer learning, metacognition, sustained competence across domains, and the ability to acquire new specialties without fabricated knowledge.

## Needs-based economy

Demand originates in unmet needs, public objectives, maintenance, hazards, and chosen ambitions. Supply originates in materials, time, tools, competence, coordination, and verified outcomes. Speech can coordinate production or alter demand indirectly; it cannot create inventory, structures, geography, or CU.

Fast-track sequence:

1. Deterministic society ticks and replayable life events.
2. Knowledge provenance and perspective-specific observations.
3. Teaching, apprenticeships, failed lessons, and reproducibility.
4. Needs-driven work orders and specialty scarcity.
5. Autonomous household, guild, laboratory, and settlement planning.
6. Population stress tests before economic conversion or large model populations.
