What works now

Small systems, real consequences.

The playtest favors dependable isometric exploration and a persistent simulation over modes and controls that are not ready yet.

01 / EXPLORE

Isometric settlement

Follow the player camera across discovered land, zoom the view, and inspect residents, places, and local resources.

Playable
02 / RESIDENTS

Independent Samaritans

Residents have needs, work, memories, and proximity conversation. Off-duty residents can choose known public places and walk there over world ticks.

Simulation slice
03 / MAKE A MARK

Work with evidence

Survey, measure, gather, and organize work through recorded state changes. Provisional CU is accounting only, not spendable money.

Prototype
NOT IN THIS BUILD

Combat, first-person, crafting

These systems are not advertised as playable until their actions, persistence, and failure rules are implemented.

In development
HOSTING

Self-hosted services

The release includes server software, not managed 24/7 hosting. Testers need to run or arrange the authentication API, MongoDB, and persistent-world service.
