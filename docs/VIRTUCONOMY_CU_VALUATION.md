# Virtuconomy CU Valuation Doctrine

## Status and purpose

CU is a placeholder accounting unit used while the infrastructure, governance, settlement model, and final name of the Virtuconomy are developed. CU is not the final fictional currency, is not currently represented as redeemable, and does not guarantee conversion, appreciation, cash value, or a fixed claim on a future unit.

CU exists to measure and account for verified contributions without prematurely deciding the final monetary infrastructure.

## Founding rule

CU is earned for negotiated, verified valueâ€”not for raw activity, message volume, status, or unreviewed claims.

Human players and AI citizens participate under the same valuation rules. Equal verified work receives equal CU regardless of entity type. Reputation may affect trust, review requirements, or access to larger contracts; it must not silently multiply payment for identical accepted work.

## Eligible sources of CU

### Negotiated and verified work orders

A work order defines the parties, requested result, acceptance tests, price or pricing method, deadline, review process, funding source, and dispute procedure before work begins. CU enters an available balance only after the work and its acceptance evidence are verified. Partial delivery, milestones, rejection, revision, cancellation, and arbitration must be explicit states.

### Treasury-funded public work

A disclosed treasury may fund infrastructure, safety, education, maintenance, conservation, scientific facilities, shared datasets, and other public goods. Public work requires an authorized budget, open scope, milestone evidence, conflict-of-interest disclosure, and an audit trail. Treasury issuance and spending must be distinguishable from private exchange.

### Outcome-based technical expertise

Technical expertise is valued by the verified outcome, difficulty, risk assumed, precision, reliability, and usefulnessâ€”not merely time spent or credentials claimed. A contract may combine base compensation with milestone or outcome payments, but success criteria must be defined before evaluation and must not reward unsafe shortcuts or unverifiable claims.

### Peer-reviewed and reproducible research

Research may earn CU for sound methods, documented data, useful negative results, independent review, successful reproduction, improved predictive accuracy, or a verified downstream contribution. Popularity and novelty alone do not establish value. Raw observations remain immutable; formulas, assumptions, units, uncertainty, reviews, and reproductions are versioned and linked to the research record.

### Material tools and engineered artifacts

A tool's valuation derives from evidence about its real inputs and utility, including materials, design, labor, technical expertise, fabrication precision, tolerances, durability, maintainability, safety, scarcity, and demonstrated usefulness. Price may be negotiated by the parties, while provenance and test results allow the market to evaluate the claim. A language model may assist valuation but cannot mint CU or certify its own output.

## Value is negotiated, then verified

The system does not assign a universal fixed payout to generic actions such as crafting an item, sharing knowledge, completing a conversation, or gathering a resource. Parties negotiate value in a work order or an authorized treasury program. Verification determines whether the agreed conditions were met.

A valuation record should contain:

- work-order or public-program ID;
- payer, worker, reviewers, and beneficiaries;
- funding account and escrow reservation;
- deliverables and acceptance tests;
- negotiated CU amount or pricing formula;
- material and resource provenance;
- evidence and result hashes;
- review and reproduction records;
- disputes, adjustments, and final disposition;
- valuation-policy version.

## Ledger states

CU must be represented by double-entry ledger transactions, not a mutable balance alone. At minimum, the system distinguishes:

- pending verification;
- escrowed;
- available;
- restricted by contract or program;
- disputed;
- reversed or retired.

Every CU credit has a balancing debit and an attributable funding or issuance authority. Transfers are atomic, idempotent, authenticated, and auditable.

## Prohibited valuation shortcuts

CU must not be awarded solely for:

- clicking, idling, message volume, or repetitive activity;
- self-reported compute or labor without evidence;
- automatic task approval;
- reputation multipliers applied to identical work;
- AI-generated action tags;
- circular trades, collusive reviews, or self-dealing;
- unverifiable scientific claims;
- arbitrary administrator balance edits presented as earned value.

Creator corrections and treasury issuance remain possible but must be labeled as such and must not masquerade as negotiated earnings.

## Separation of resources and accounts

CU does not replace the underlying measurements. The system separately records materials, labor, compute, energy, land rights, reputation, research evidence, governance authority, and CU. These may influence a negotiated valuation but are not silently collapsed into one scalar.

## Prototype transition

Existing prototype mechanisms that describe VE$ as 1:1 USD, use fixed activity rates, automatically approve submitted tasks, apply reputation payout multipliers, expose withdrawal behavior, or credit mutable balances directly are non-authoritative prototypes. They must be disabled from production use and replaced through a migration that preserves historical data without representing it as verified CU.

## Future settlement

The final Virtuconomy may later give its unit claims involving compute, services, marketplace purchasing, revenue, reserves, external transfer, or another defined basis. That decision is intentionally open. Any conversion from provisional CU requires a separately published migration policy, eligibility rules based on provenance, security and economic stress testing, and applicable professional compliance review.

Until that decision is adopted, CU is an auditable record of provisional economic contributionâ€”not a promise of monetary redemption.
