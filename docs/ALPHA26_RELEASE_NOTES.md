# Echoes of Virtuality Alpha.26

## Witnessed discourse becomes persistent social causality

Conversation no longer ends at an NPC reply. Civic concerns about water, food, storage, or sanitation can become durable claims when nearby autonomous residents hear them.

- Speech remains non-physical and cannot directly alter terrain, facilities, inventory, or resources.
- Repeating the same concern within one world tick counts once, preventing request spam from manufacturing instant pressure.
- A topic requires statements on three distinct ticks and at least two NPC witnesses before Sora records an escalation.
- Each escalation preserves its source messages, speakers, witnesses, topic, and pressure contribution.
- Public pressure modifies a later council evidence review rather than bypassing deliberation.
- If the council selects the concern, physical change still requires the normal public-work lifecycle: acceptance, supply, work, evidence, and inspection.
- Claims, escalations, council knowledge, decisions, and later consequences persist through server restart.

The persistent world schema advances to 18. Existing worlds migrate in place without losing prior messages, memories, consequences, or institutional history.
